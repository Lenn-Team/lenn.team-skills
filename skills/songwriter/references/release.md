# Release (focus: Spotify)

Research status: November 2026. Platform and distributor rules change quickly – check the current help pages before every release. Claude is not a lawyer; for commercial projects or uncertainty about rights, get professional advice. Copyright and collecting-society rules differ by country – the notes below cover the general picture plus Germany as an example.

## 1. Basics
- Music only reaches Spotify through a **distributor** (or a label). There is no direct upload.
- The distributor assigns ISRC (per track) and UPC (per release) and delivers to Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon, Deezer, YouTube Music, TikTok etc.
- After delivery, claim the artist profile in **Spotify for Artists** (artists.spotify.com).

## 2. Rights first
**Suno:** only songs created during an active Pro or Premier subscription may be exploited commercially; a later subscription does not apply retroactively. Since the Warner settlement Suno speaks of granted commercial-use rights; copyright protection is not guaranteed.
**ElevenLabs:** release on streaming services from the Creator plan according to comparison sites; check the plan terms.
**Copyright (e.g. Germany, similar in the US):** only what a human has creatively shaped is protected. Self-written lyrics are protected as a literary work; purely AI-generated music has no author. Therefore:
- Document your own share of the lyrics (brief, drafts, decisions, revisions).
- Credit only real human contributions as composer/lyricist.
- **Collecting societies (e.g. GEMA in Germany):** registering a work requires human authorship. For AI-generated music with your own lyrics, check the society's current rules on AI shares or ask them.
**No imitation:** voices that clearly sound like real artists, sound-alikes and "feat." credits for people who weren't involved lead to removal.

## 3. Choosing a distributor (status Nov 2026, according to distributor pages and trade reports)

| Distributor | AI-generated music |
|---|---|
| DistroKid | allowed if you hold 100 % of the rights and disclose AI; since Nov 2026 additional AI detection (ACRCloud tag) |
| TuneCore / Believe | since April 2026 blocks tracks from unlicensed generators like Suno or 100 % AI-generated tracks |
| CD Baby | AI only as a tool, generated content not allowed |
| Others (Amuse, RouteNote, Ditto …) | check the current policy |

Bandcamp has not accepted predominantly AI-generated music since January 2026. Deezer labels and filters AI music actively; TIDAL pays no royalties for tracks detected as fully AI-generated.

**Don't recommend** tools or tricks to hide AI origin or get around detection systems. That breaks distributor and platform rules and can lead to a ban.

## 4. Disclose AI involvement
Spotify uses the DDEX standard for AI information in credits (visible in the app since April 2026). It is declared via the distributor, split into categories such as **vocals, instrumentation, composition, post-production, lyrics**. According to Spotify, the declaration is no reason for downranking; the Spotify rules target spam, fraud and voice impersonation.

A typical honest classification for a song made with this skill:
- Lyrics: written by a human, with AI assistance (editing/suggestions) – depending on the share
- Vocals: AI-generated
- Instrumentation/composition: AI-generated
- Post-production: depending

Don't call a fully generated track "AI-assisted" just because title or version were chosen.

## 5. Material

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Audio | WAV/FLAC in the best possible quality (Suno: use the WAV download); loudness for streaming approx. −14 LUFS integrated, −1 dBTP (platforms normalise) |
| Cover | square, at least 3000 × 3000 px, JPG or PNG, RGB; no URLs, prices, store logos, no third-party brands/people without rights |
| Metadata | title, artist name (consistent!), version (e.g. "acoustic"), main genre/subgenre, language, explicit yes/no, composer(s), lyricist, producer, release date, label name (or your own), ©/℗ line, AI information |
| Lyrics | sing-along lyrics in correct spelling; Spotify lyrics currently come via Musixmatch – submit/verify the lyrics there |

Cover: an AI-generated cover is subject to the image generator's terms; Apple Music increasingly expects AI information for artwork.

## 6. Timeline
- **4 weeks before:** upload the release with final audio, cover and metadata to the distributor.
- **2–3 weeks before:** the release appears in Spotify for Artists under *Music → Upcoming*.
- **At least 7 days before (better 10–14):** submit the song to the editors in Spotify for Artists ("pitch"). A song pitched on time lands in followers' Release Radar playlists. Only one unreleased song can be pitched at a time; a pitch does not guarantee playlist placement.
- **Release day:** profile up to date, Canvas/clip (optional), share on your own channels.
- **Afterwards:** lyrics on Musixmatch, watch statistics in Spotify for Artists.

## 7. Pitch text
Short, specific, without "will go viral": what it is about (one sentence), genre/mood/instruments, what's special (story, occasion, region), marketing (video, gig, collaboration). Choose genre, mood and culture tags precisely.

## 8. Don'ts and risks
- No bought streams, bot plays or "guaranteed playlist" offers.
- No mass uploads of similar tracks.
- No voice or style impersonation of real artists.
- Complete chain of rights: generator plan, own lyrics, cover rights, samples if any.
