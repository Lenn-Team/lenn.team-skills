# Market and trend research

Trends are researched at runtime, never from memory. This document describes the method, not the current state.

## When
- For every song that is to be released or meant to "land".
- Not needed for purely private occasion songs, unless the user wants it.

## Sources (check in this order)
1. **Spotify Charts** (charts.spotify.com): Top 50 and Viral 50 for the target country and global.
2. **National charts** (e.g. Offizielle Deutsche Charts, UK Official Charts, Billboard for the US).
3. **TikTok**: Creative Center / trend overviews for sounds and songs in the target country.
4. **Genre-specific**: editorial playlists of the platforms for the target genre, music press (e.g. Billboard, Pitchfork, Music Business Worldwide, regional magazines).
5. **YouTube**: music trends in the target country.

Work with web search and page fetch; don't work around pages that cannot be fetched – use another source.

## What to extract
For 10–20 current songs of the target genre or market:
- Tempo range and feel (halftime, four-on-the-floor, swing)
- Song length, intro length, time to the first hook
- Dominant sounds/instruments and production aesthetic (e.g. dry and close, 2000s revival, live band)
- Vocal styles (spoken, breathy, belting, auto-tune as an effect)
- Topics and attitude (irony, vulnerability, party, home)
- Language and language mix
- Recurring form features (post-chorus hooks, ad-libs, chants, breakdown before the last chorus)

Put the result in the brief as a short table with date and sources.

## Decide the stance
Agree on one of three stances with the user:
- **Follow the trend:** adopt form features and sound aesthetic, keep content and language your own. Risk: interchangeable and quickly dated.
- **Deliberately against it:** contrast as profile (e.g. acoustic, long, narrative in a glossy phase). Must clearly be intentional.
- **Timeless:** classic form, few time-bound production markers.

## Limits
- Trends describe what is playing now, not what will work. Success depends heavily on reach, artist and chance.
- Don't rebuild melodies, hooks or distinctive lyric lines of current hits.
