# Research

Research has three jobs: sharpen the musical direction, know the market, and keep the content true.

## 1. Musical direction
- Start from the genre profile in `genres.md`, then adapt to references.
- Translate reference songs into traits per `audio-references.md` (tempo, key/mode, instruments, vocals, production, era, structure).
- Result: a trait profile, not a list of names. Artist names stay in the notes, never in the generator prompt.

## 2. Market
Per `trends.md`. Always record date and source – trends go stale within weeks.

## 3. Content
- **Specialist topics** (professions, technology, sport, medicine, rescue services …): use terms correctly, jargon sparingly – one precise term sounds credible, three sound like a manual.
- **Places:** real street names, landmarks and dialect words add authenticity. Check that they exist and how they are pronounced.
- **Real events and people** – documentary rules:
  1. Narrator in the third person, unless the person tells it themselves (with consent).
  2. Direct quotes only from sources, with a record in the notes.
  3. Inner states (fear, thoughts) only if a source documents them.
  4. Actions only if documented.
  5. No factual claim beyond what is documented. A missing source is not a statement.
  For crimes, accidents and deceased people: be especially restrained, think of relatives, alienate the material when in doubt.
- **Occasion songs:** only facts the user provided. Exaggeration only where the user allowed it.

## 4. Record sources
In the brief (section "Sources/notes") with title, link and access date. Write down the key statements briefly in your own words, because links disappear.

## 5. What is not researched
- Lyrics of other artists in order to rebuild them.
- Ways to hide AI origin or to get around distributors' detection systems.
